An egg hatches… and a baby dinosaur becomes your child's learning buddy! DinoLearn offers short educational games for children aged 3 to 7 that adapt to their age and level.

LEARN THROUGH PLAY
• Maths: counting, comparing, adding, telling the time, money, sharing a cake (halves, thirds, quarters)
• Logic: odd one out, patterns, shapes and colours, memory, fruit sudoku, programming the dino with arrows
• Spanish: first words, numbers, memory in Spanish
• Discovering the world: animals, shadows, your country
• Writing: tracing shapes, letters and numbers with a finger
• Speaking games: the child answers out loud and the dino listens

A DINO TO RAISE
Every right answer earns fruit. Your child feeds the dino, watches it grow, and wins clothes and surprises to dress it up.

MADE FOR PARENTS
• Parents' area protected by a code
• Daily play time and bedtime
• Progress tracking, game selection, several children on one device

SAFE AND RESPECTFUL
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
• No data collected: everything stays on the device
• Speech recognition runs on the device: nothing is recorded or sent
• Works offline after the first download (dino voice and speech recognition, Wi-Fi recommended)

Available in 20 languages.
